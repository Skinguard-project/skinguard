BEST PROJECT · 1 OF 35 TEAMS

When the arm could no longer grip,
I made it wave.

Forty minutes before our final demonstration, a wire came loose inside the robot body. The arm could no longer grip. Opening the main body risked creating more problems.

While our team debated securing the arm or concealing the failure, I tried another approach: rewriting the code so the arm moved between 120 and 150 degrees. The robot could wave, even though it could no longer grip.

Our project was selected as Best Project among 35 teams. The experience taught me to demonstrate a workable next step when discussion alone stops moving a team forward.
